# 三角函数、三角形相关公式

## 平方和

令

$$
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
$$

有

$$
\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha=1
$$

## 正切定义

令

$$
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
$$

有

$$
\tan\alpha=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}
$$

## 诱导公式

令

$$
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}\\\\k\in\mathbb{Z}
$$

有

### 一

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(\alpha+k\cdot2\pi)&=\sin\alpha\tag{诱导1s}\\\\
\cos(\alpha+k\cdot2\pi)&=\cos\alpha\tag{诱导1c}\\\\
\tan(\alpha+k\cdot2\pi)&=\tan\alpha\tag{诱导1t}
\end{align}
$$

### 二

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(\pi+\alpha)&=-\sin\alpha\tag{诱导2s}\\\\
\cos(\pi+\alpha)&=-\cos\alpha\tag{诱导2c}\\\\
\tan(\pi+\alpha)&=\tan\alpha\tag{诱导2t}
\end{align}
$$

### 三

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(-\alpha)&=-\sin\alpha\tag{诱导3s}\\\\
\cos(-\alpha)&=\cos\alpha\tag{诱导3c}\\\\
\tan(-\alpha)&=-\tan\alpha\tag{诱导3t}
\end{align}
$$

### 四

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(\pi-\alpha)&=\sin\alpha\tag{诱导4s}\\\\
\cos(\pi-\alpha)&=-\cos\alpha\tag{诱导4c}\\\\
\tan(\pi-\alpha)&=-\tan\alpha\tag{诱导4t}
\end{align}
$$

### 五

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}-\alpha)=\cos\alpha\tag{诱导5s}\\\\
\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}-\alpha)=\sin\alpha\tag{诱导5c}
\end{align}
$$

### 六

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha)&=\cos\alpha\tag{诱导6s}\\\\
\cos(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha)&=-\sin\alpha\tag{诱导6c}
\end{align}
$$

## 和差角

令

$$
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}\\\\\beta\in\mathbb{R}
$$

有

### 差角

$$
\begin{align}
\sin(\alpha-\beta)&=\sin\alpha\cos\beta-\cos\alpha\sin\beta\tag{差角s}\\\\
\cos(\alpha-\beta)&=\cos\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta\tag{差角c}\\\\
\tan(\alpha-\beta)&=\frac{\tan\alpha-\tan\beta}{1+\tan\alpha\tan\beta}\tag{差角t}
\end{align}
$$

### 和角

$$
\begin{align}
\cos(\alpha+\beta)&=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta\tag{和角s}\\\\
\sin(\alpha+\beta)&=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta\tag{和角c}\\\\
\tan(\alpha+\beta)&=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\beta}\tag{和角t}
\end{align}
$$

## 二倍角、半角

令

$$
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}
$$

有

$$
\begin{align}
\sin2\alpha&=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\tag{二倍s}\\\\
\cos2\alpha&=\cos^2\alpha-\sin^2\alpha\tag{二倍c}\\\\
\tan2\alpha&=\frac{2\tan\alpha}{1-\tan^2\alpha}\tag{二倍t}
\end{align}
$$

其中，对于“二倍c”，把平方和等于1的式子带入，得

$$
\begin{align}
\cos2\alpha&=1-2\sin^2\alpha\tag{二倍c1}\\\\
&=2\cos^2\alpha-1\tag{二倍c2}
\end{align}
$$

### 降幂扩角

$$
\begin{align}
\sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}&=\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}\tag{半角1s}\\\\
\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2}&=\frac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}\tag{半角1c}\\\\
\tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}&=\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{1+\cos2\alpha}\tag{半角1t}
\end{align}
$$

### “小万能”

$$
\begin{align}
\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}&=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}}\tag{半角2s}\\\\
\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}&=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}}\tag{半角2c}\\\\
\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}&=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{1+\cos\alpha}}\tag{半角2t}
\end{align}
$$

其中正负号由$\dfrac{\alpha}{2}$所在象限决定。

### “大万能”

$$
\begin{align}
\sin2\alpha&=\frac{2\tan\alpha}{1+\tan\alpha}\tag{二倍2s}\\\\
\cos2\alpha&=\frac{1-\tan\alpha}{1+\tan\alpha}\tag{二倍2c}\\\\
\tan2\alpha&=\frac{2\tan\alpha}{1-\tan\alpha}\tag{二倍2t}
\end{align}
$$

## 积化和差、和差化积

令

$$
\alpha\in\mathbb{R}\\\\\beta\in\mathbb{R}\\\\\theta\in\mathbb{R}\\\\\varphi\in\mathbb{R}
$$

有

### 积化和差

$$
\begin{align}
\sin\alpha\cos\beta&=\frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)]\tag{sc}\\\\
\cos\alpha\sin\beta&=\frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha+\beta)-\sin(\alpha-\beta)]\tag{cs}\\\\
\cos\alpha\cos\beta&=\frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha+\beta)+\cos(\alpha-\beta)]\tag{cc}\\\\
\sin\alpha\sin\beta&=-\frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha+\beta)-\cos(\alpha-\beta)]\tag{ss}
\end{align}
$$

### 和差化积

$$
\begin{align}
\sin\theta+\sin\varphi&=2\sin\frac{\theta+\varphi}{2}\cos\frac{\theta-\varphi}{2}\tag{s+s}\\\\
\sin\theta-\sin\varphi&=2\cos\frac{\theta+\varphi}{2}\sin\frac{\theta-\varphi}{2}\tag{s-s}\\\\
\cos\theta+\cos\varphi&=2\cos\frac{\theta+\varphi}{2}\cos\frac{\theta-\varphi}{2}\tag{c+c}\\\\
\cos\theta-\cos\varphi&=-2\sin\frac{\theta+\varphi}{2}\sin\frac{\theta-\varphi}{2}\tag{c-c}
\end{align}
$$

## 辅助角

令

$$
x\in\mathbb{R}\\\\y\in\mathbb{R}\\\\\theta\in\mathbb{R}
$$

有

$$
\begin{align}
x\sin\theta+y\cos\theta
&=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}(\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\sin\theta+\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\cos\theta)\tag{原理}\\\\
&=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\sin(\theta+\arctan\frac{y}{x})\tag{s}\\\\
&=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\cos(\theta+\arctan\frac{x}{y})\tag{c}
\end{align}
$$

### 化为$\dfrac{\pi}{3}$的

$$
\begin{align}
\sin\theta\pm\sqrt{3}\cos\theta&=2sin(\theta\pm\frac{\pi}{3})\tag{s}\\\\
\sqrt{3}\sin\theta\pm\cos\theta&=-2\cos(\theta\mp\frac{\pi}{3})\tag{c}
\end{align}
$$

### 化为$\dfrac{\pi}{4}$的

$$
\begin{align}
\sin\theta\pm\cos\theta&=\sqrt{2}\sin(\theta\pm\frac{\pi}{4})\tag{s}\\\\
\sin\theta\pm\cos\theta&=-\sqrt{2}\cos(\theta\mp\frac{\pi}{4})\tag{c}
\end{align}
$$

### 化为$\dfrac{\pi}{6}$的

$$
\begin{align}
\sqrt{3}\sin\theta\pm\cos\theta&=2\sin(\theta\pm\frac{\pi}{6})\tag{s}\\\\
\sin\theta\pm\sqrt{3}\cos\theta&=-2\cos(\theta\pm\frac{\pi}{6})\tag{c}
\end{align}
$$

## 三角形

令

$$
A\in(0,\pi)\\\\B\in(0,\pi)\\\\C\in(0,\pi)\\\\A+B+C=\pi
$$

有

$$
\begin{align}\sin A&=\sin(B+C)\tag{sin A}\\\\\sin B&=\sin(A+C)\tag{sin B}\\\\\sin C&=\sin(A+B)\tag{sin C}\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}\cos A&=-\cos(B+C)\tag{cos A}\\\\\cos B&=-\cos(A+C)\tag{cos B}\\\\\cos C&=-\cos(A+B)\tag{cos C}\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align} 
\tan A&=-\tan(B+C)\tag{tan A}\\\\
\tan B&=-\tan(A+C)\tag{tan B}\\\\
\tan C&=-\tan(A+B)\tag{tan C}
\end{align}
$$

$$
\tan A\cdot\tan B\cdot\tan C=\tan A+\tan B+\tan C\tag{tan ABC}
$$
